# Bundled card artwork

Source: https://github.com/hzrqftr/yugioh-forbiddenmemories
Pinned revision: c50e070636fed6a0b2a0d6a9f2f88f79f07d58c2

All 722 images are mapped by Forbidden Memories card number. WebP images were decoded and saved as PNG without cropping, resizing, or changing their pixels, for Windows/WPF compatibility. The original per-card image-source manifest is retained in card_images.json; conversion hashes and dimensions are in manifest.json. Kuwagata alpha (card 480) is named Kuwagata a in the companion catalog; the card number and identity match.

Modified conversion-manifest metadata is likewise available under CC BY-SA 4.0. No ownership of the underlying artwork is claimed.

The companion reads these files locally at startup and does not contact GitHub or Fandom. Optional custom images take precedence; absent or unreadable overrides fall back to the bundled artwork.
